Not one issue. #4471 fits one of the two problems. The other had no ticket of its own.

How we counted

Each report is placed by its tenant: the locale it writes dates in and the booking form it runs. The intake tag is a guess made from the reporter’s browser, and it is wrong for dozens of reports. The export also repeats some rows, so we count each report id once.

The two problems

  1. The shared parser reads dates month first. Every tenant that writes the day first sees its dates flip. This is the problem #4471 describes. It is the large majority of the reports. The desk read a few of them as a one-day move (see the note under the table); they are filed here by tenant. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.
  2. The booking-v3 form tells day-first tenants to type MM/DD/YYYY. People type what the hint says and the form reads it their way. This is a different bug: fixing the parser does not fix it. Three tenants run this form. It was merged into #4471 by mistake. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired.

Each problem now has its own ticket in the #4471 follow-up repo, with its report count.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is mostly one bug reported many times, until it is not. Check the cause before you merge, not only the subject (decision 0001 merges on subject alone).
  • Do not trust the intake tag. Look up the tenant.
